FITNESS INFORMATION

What Type of Workout Is Good for You?

Different types of physical activity provide different fitness benefits.
💪

Strength Training

Resistance exercises can help improve muscular strength and support everyday physical activities.

Examples

Squats, push-ups, rows, presses

❤️

Cardiovascular Exercise

Cardio activities can help improve cardiovascular fitness and endurance.

Examples

Walking, jogging, cycling, swimming

🧘

Flexibility & Mobility

Mobility and flexibility activities can help maintain comfortable movement and range of motion.

Examples

Stretching, mobility drills, yoga

🔥

Full Body Fitness

Combining strength, cardio, and mobility can provide a balanced approach to fitness.

Examples

Circuit training, walking, bodyweight exercises
